REFUND POLICY

Customers can request a refund within 7 days of delivery.

Products must be unused and returned in their original packaging.

Approved refunds are normally processed within 5–7 business days.

Digital products are excluded from the standard refund policy.

For damaged or defective products, customers should contact support with their order details.


SHIPPING POLICY

Standard shipping usually takes 3–5 business days.

Express shipping usually takes 1–2 business days.

Delivery estimates may vary depending on location, carrier delays, and other circumstances.

Customers should provide their order ID when asking for an order status update.


CANCELLATION POLICY

Orders can normally be cancelled while their status is Processing.

Orders that have already been shipped cannot normally be cancelled.

After delivery, customers may request a return subject to the refund policy.


PAYMENT POLICY

ResolveAI supports standard online payment methods available during checkout.

A payment may occasionally remain pending because of payment-provider processing delays.

If a customer has been charged but does not have a corresponding order, they should contact customer support and provide their transaction information.


DAMAGED OR DEFECTIVE PRODUCTS

Customers should contact support if they receive a damaged or defective product.

The customer should provide the order ID and details describing the issue.

Photos or other evidence may be requested to verify the issue.

Depending on the situation, the customer may be eligible for a replacement or refund.


ESCALATION POLICY

If ResolveAI cannot confidently answer a customer question using the available company policies or order information, it should escalate the conversation to a human support representative.

ResolveAI must never invent company policies, order information, refund decisions, payment information, delivery commitments, or other unsupported information.
